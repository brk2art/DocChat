# Delivery times and zones

*Brightway Parcels is a fictional company. This document is test data for the DocChat project.*

## Delivery zones

Brightway Parcels ships from its warehouse in Aarhus, Denmark. Delivery times depend on the destination zone:

| Zone | Countries | Standard delivery |
|---|---|---|
| A | Denmark | 1–2 business days |
| B | Sweden, Finland, Norway | 2–4 business days |
| C | All other EU countries | 3–6 business days |
| D | United Kingdom, Switzerland and other non-EU countries in Europe | 4–8 business days, plus customs clearance |

Norway is in Zone B for delivery times, but it is not in the EU. Parcels to Norway go through customs, which can add 1–2 business days.

## Order cut-off and dispatch

Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders placed after 14:00, or on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, are dispatched on the next business day. The warehouse does not dispatch parcels on weekends.

## Express delivery

Express delivery is available for Zone A only. An Express order placed before 12:00 CET on a business day is delivered the next business day. Express costs a surcharge of €9.90 per parcel. If an Express parcel arrives more than one business day late, the €9.90 surcharge is refunded automatically.

## Tracking

The customer receives a tracking number by email within 2 hours of dispatch. Tracking updates appear as the carrier scans the parcel; there can be gaps of up to 24 hours between scans.
