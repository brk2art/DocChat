# Damaged parcels and claims

*Brightway Parcels is a fictional company. This document is test data for the DocChat project.*

## When to report damage

A damaged parcel must be reported within 14 calendar days of delivery. After 14 days, damage claims cannot be accepted. This also applies to concealed damage: if the box looked fine but the contents were broken when opened, the 14-day limit still counts from the delivery date.

## How to file a claim

Claims are filed through the claim form in the customer portal. Claims sent by email are not processed. The claim must include:

- photos of the outer packaging, showing the damage;
- photos of the damaged contents;
- a photo of the shipping label;
- the order number.

Keep the original packaging until the claim is closed. The carrier may ask to inspect it.

## Compensation

Compensation covers the declared value of the contents, up to €500 per parcel for standard shipping. Parcels sent with Insured Shipping are covered up to €2,500. Shipping costs are refunded as well when a claim is approved.

## Decision and refund

Brightway Parcels decides on a claim within 10 business days of receiving a complete claim. An approved refund is paid to the original payment method within 5 business days of the decision. If the claim is rejected, the decision explains why, and the customer can appeal once within 30 days.
