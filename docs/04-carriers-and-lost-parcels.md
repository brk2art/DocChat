# Carriers, service levels and lost parcels

*Brightway Parcels is a fictional company. This document is test data for the DocChat project.*

## Our carriers

| Carrier | Used for | On-time target | Tracking updates |
|---|---|---|---|
| FastShip | Express, and standard parcels in Zone A | 97% | Every scan, usually within minutes |
| NordPost | Standard parcels to Zone B | 94% | Every 4 hours |
| ParcelGo | Standard parcels to Zones C and D | 90% | Every scan, pushed in real time |

The on-time target is the share of parcels each carrier must deliver within the standard delivery time for the zone. Carrier performance is reviewed every quarter.

## When a parcel seems stuck

If a parcel shows no tracking update for 3 business days, contact customer service. Brightway Parcels then opens a lost-parcel investigation with the carrier.

## Lost parcels

A parcel is declared lost if the investigation has not found it 15 business days after it was opened. A lost parcel is compensated like a damage claim: the declared value up to €500, or up to €2,500 with Insured Shipping, plus the shipping cost. The customer can choose a refund or a replacement, if the item is in stock.

## Wrong address

If the customer entered a wrong address, the carrier returns the parcel to the warehouse. Brightway Parcels can resend it to a corrected address for the normal shipping fee. Parcels delivered to a wrong address entered by the customer are not covered as lost.
