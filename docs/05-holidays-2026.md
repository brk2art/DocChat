# Holiday and peak season schedule 2026

*Brightway Parcels is a fictional company. This document is test data for the DocChat project.*

## Peak season

Peak season runs from Black Friday, 27 November 2026, to 31 December 2026. During peak season, standard delivery times in all zones can be 1–2 business days longer than usual. Express delivery in Zone A keeps its next-business-day promise.

## Last order dates for delivery before Christmas

To arrive before 24 December 2026 with standard delivery, orders must be placed by:

| Zone | Last order date |
|---|---|
| A (Denmark) | 19 December 2026 |
| B (Sweden, Finland, Norway) | 17 December 2026 |
| C (other EU countries) | 14 December 2026 |
| D (UK, Switzerland, other non-EU) | 10 December 2026 |

Express orders in Zone A placed by 22 December 2026 before 12:00 CET arrive by 23 December.

## Warehouse closing days

The warehouse is closed and dispatches nothing on 24, 25 and 26 December 2026 and on 1 January 2027. On 31 December 2026 the warehouse closes at 12:00, and orders placed after 11:00 are dispatched on 4 January 2027.

## Customer service over the holidays

Customer service is closed on 24, 25 and 26 December and on 1 January. On 31 December it is open from 09:00 to 12:00 CET.
