# Prohibited items, weight and size limits

*Brightway Parcels is a fictional company. This document is test data for the DocChat project.*

## Items we do not ship

- lithium batteries on their own (spare batteries, power banks);
- aerosols and spray cans;
- flammable liquids, such as lighter fluid or paint thinner;
- cash, cheques and gift vouchers with a cash value;
- live animals and plants.

Devices with a lithium battery built in, such as phones and laptops, can be shipped if the battery is under 100 Wh and the device is switched off.

## Alcohol, food and perfume

Alcohol is never shipped to Norway. Norway is in Zone B for delivery times, but it is outside the EU.

For every other destination: food, alcohol and perfume can be sent to Zones A, B and C without extra paperwork, and to Zone D with a customs declaration.

## Weight and size

The maximum weight is 30 kg per parcel. The maximum length is 120 cm on the longest side. Parcels between 120 cm and 175 cm long are accepted for an oversize surcharge of €15. Parcels over 175 cm or over 30 kg are not accepted.
