# Customer service and privacy

*Brightway Parcels is a fictional company. This document is test data for the DocChat project.*

## Opening hours

Customer service is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 18:00 CET, and Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00 CET. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Live chat in the customer portal has the same hours. Messages sent outside opening hours are answered on the next working day.

## Response times

Chat is answered within 5 minutes during opening hours. Messages through the contact form are answered within 1 business day. If a case has no answer after 48 hours, the customer can ask for it to be escalated to a supervisor.

## Changing an order

An order can be changed or cancelled until it is dispatched. After dispatch, the parcel cannot be stopped or redirected; the customer can return it after delivery instead.

## Personal data

Brightway Parcels keeps order and address data for 5 years for accounting purposes. Customers can request a copy of their personal data, or its deletion where the law allows, by writing to privacy@brightway-parcels.example. Requests are answered within 30 days.
